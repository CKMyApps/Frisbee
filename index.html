THE QUICK GUIDE

How to Play

01 / AIM

Tap or click the course to aim. Fine-tune with the on-screen controls, the left and right arrows, or A and D.

02 / THROW

Hold THROW or Space to build power, then release. Use less power near the basket and allow for the wind.

03 / SCORE

Sink the disc in the chain basket. Water and out-of-bounds cost an extra stroke. Lowest total after all three holes wins.

Your unfinished round is saved in this browser. Local multiplayer uses one shared screen.
