Mouse or touch to aim; A/D or arrow keys to fine-tune.
Hold THROW or Space to charge, then release.
Solo, vs CPU, and local pass-and-play. No online multiplayer.
Progress is saved in the browser when local storage is available.
The full Neon Rim icon (1024 x 1024) is inside assets.

New rounds: Original (Pine Glade, Willow Bend, Copper Ridge), then Street, then Rooftop. Parked cars and street furniture can stop low shots. Rooftop wind is strong; cross gaps in flight and land on a roof. A missed roof costs one stroke and returns the disc to its last lie. Old saved rounds still resume on the original three-hole layout. Color and Monochrome continue to keep separate progress.
